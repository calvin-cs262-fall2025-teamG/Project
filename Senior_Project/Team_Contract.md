# Hey, Neighbor! Team Contract

Our team consists of Bryn Lamppa, Gregory Goodfellow, and Jacob Foote. We meet as a team on Thursday nights and check in with our advisor, Professor Norman, every Tuesday. Each member commits to roughly three hours of individual work per week, and we stay in touch between meetings over Messages and Teams.

We use GitHub Flow for our development work: all changes go on feature branches and are merged into main through pull requests that at least one other teammate reviews. Major decisions are made by consensus, and if we can't agree, we take a simple majority vote.

If a conflict comes up, we'll handle it directly and calmly between the people involved first. If that doesn't resolve it, we bring it to the full team for an open discussion, and if we still can't work it out, we escalate the issue to Professor Norman for mediation. During disagreements, we agree to focus on the issue, not the person, and to listen to each other.

Everyone is responsible for finishing their assigned tasks on time and flagging any missed deadlines early so the team can adjust. We hold each other accountable to the project's values around privacy, fairness, and keeping borrowing human-to-human by asking each other directly rather than letting problems pile up.
